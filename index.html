Electricity spot prices - SE4

Prices load automatically for the weather/solar simulation period and are used in hourly results. Prices exclude VAT, supplier markup, energy tax and network charges.

Prices supplied by Elprisetjustnu.se. Original interval values are retained; quarter-hour prices become duration-weighted hourly averages. Applying these to hourly energy assumes uniform use within each hour. Invoice-backed variable import charges are integrated; export contract adjustments remain unverified.

Solar model assumptions

Temperature model: Faiman, using 10 m wind as an approximation for wind at the panels. Power uses a linear temperature coefficient. Constant inverter efficiency and other losses are assumptions, not verified product ratings. Local shading and panel snow coverage are excluded. Cloud effects are already included in sunlight.
